Task requirements & full brief

BlenderWorld is a benchmark of 223 Blender tasks scored by its own evaluator with cloud judges. This challenge uses one of its tasks and does not run that evaluator, so its results are not BlenderWorld scores. Runs are scored with hidden acceptance cases read from final.blend (sizes, materials, camera, lights and the turn over its frame range) and from the video (format, frame count, the loop and the backdrop); revision 2 of the cases accepts both readings the task allows for the body height and the label area. Each run is also read by hand against BlenderWorld's 8 public rubric criteria for the task, which is still not a BlenderWorld score. The language model and Cycles share the Mac's 64 GB. Taste is judged by eye. No transcripts are published.

  • A frosted light-cyan glass body 40&nbsp;mm across and 105&nbsp;mm tall with a rounded shoulder, a 24&nbsp;mm neck and a brushed-silver press-pump, about 150&nbsp;mm closed.
  • A blank 50&times;40&nbsp;mm label, soft three-point lighting and a light-gray-to-white seamless backdrop.
  • A 50&nbsp;mm camera at a slightly top-down 3/4 view, and one turn around Z at constant speed: 6 s at 30 fps, looping.
  • A 1080&times;1080 Cycles MP4 and the editable final.blend, rebuilt from scripts under scene/.
  • Offline, in headless Blender 5.2.1 with no MCP server: the model checks its own measurements and stills.

How to read this comparison

Compare the generated builds and the checks recorded for each attempt. There is no normalised quality score, and the cloud task’s cumulative token totals and end-to-end rate include repeated context, reasoning, tool calls and waiting — they are not comparable with local per-request decode or prefill rates. The method page sets out which figures can and cannot be set against each other.

Attempts run with Tenaz used the open-source runner at github.com/aartiles/tenaz. It plans the brief into tasks, builds each in a fresh agent session and accepts it only when its checks pass and a reviewer agrees. The method page describes it and the runners before it.
