Task requirements & full brief

The asset pack was made on the same Mac with local tools: site captures with headless Chrome, a figure built with the anatomy skill, photographs from Qwen-Image 2.1 and a VoxCPM2 narration. Renders are scored with hidden acceptance cases read from the video, including a background colour check per scene; taste is judged by a person.

  • Six scenes, each with its own background, a numbered label, a headline and footage, opening on its sentence of the narration.
  • The site's colours, Archivo and IBM Plex Mono type, framed footage with offset shadows and eased wipes.
  • Captions that stay on screen until the next sentence starts.
  • Real numbers from a recorded run, counting up, and a hidden-test score.
  • Offline, with six HyperFrames skills, including hyperframes-creative and hyperframes-keyframes.

How to read this comparison

Compare the generated builds and the checks recorded for each attempt. There is no normalised quality score, and the cloud task’s cumulative token totals and end-to-end rate include repeated context, reasoning, tool calls and waiting — they are not comparable with local per-request decode or prefill rates. The method page sets out which figures can and cannot be set against each other.
