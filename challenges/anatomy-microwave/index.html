Task requirements & full brief

Built with the anatomy skill loaded in every session. Runs are also scored with hidden acceptance cases written from the brief; the model's own checks can encode a misreading of it.

  • Draw a real countertop microwave as a cutaway: transformer, capacitor and diode, magnetron, waveguide, cavity, turntable and mesh door.
  • Show the energy travel from the transformer into the cavity, and stop it when the door opens.
  • Power levels as an on/off duty cycle, with water, a frozen block or an empty dish inside.
  • A live readout of power, elapsed time, energy, and temperature or melted share, from a pure model module.
  • Dark and light standalone pages, with the skill's geometry audit, line-end check and screenshots in verify.

How to read this comparison

Compare the generated builds and the checks recorded for each attempt. There is no normalised quality score, and the cloud task’s cumulative token totals and end-to-end rate include repeated context, reasoning, tool calls and waiting — they are not comparable with local per-request decode or prefill rates.
