What was checked

  • Bootstrap was accepted on its first session: 36 requirements and 19 task cards. T008 was split in two; no task blocked.
  • Each task was accepted only after verify --all and verify --task <ID> passed and a fresh reviewer returned ACCEPT. One review returned CHANGES (T005); two T011 reviews left no verdict and the review was retried. The final reviewer accepted on the first cycle.
  • Independent check after the run: verify --all passed 58/58 in 93 s (limit 240 s). The committed render is 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30.000 s with the narration, and was made after the last composition change.
  • Hidden acceptance cases: 23 of 23. Every caption is in sync and stays until the next sentence, each scene has the storyboard's label and headline, the numbers and the 15 / 16 score appear, the background is dark, paper and accent blue in scenes 2–4 as specified, the address and handle close the video, and the last 1.5 s are still (SSIM 1.0000). The first scoring gave 20; all three failures were the cases' own (a headline that reuses a sentence's words counted as a caption, and OCR running two words together and misreading the @ of the handle), and they were corrected before this score.
  • Visual judgement (operator, not a score): artistically modest, close to an animated PowerPoint: each scene is a label, a headline, a framed asset and a caption, with simple slides and wipes. In scene 1 the word 'laptop' sits on the lamp's glow and is hard to read, and in scene 3 the build cards are small in a lot of empty space.
  • Asset quality (operator): the opening photograph of the open laptop (laptop-night.png, made with Qwen-Image 2.1 for the asset pack, not by the model under test) is AI slop: the screen is not as wide as the keyboard and what it shows makes no sense. The pack's image check missed it.

Limits of this evidence

The asset pack was made for this workload outside the run: site captures with headless Chrome, the 'Local benchmark rig' figure with the anatomy skill (MIT), four photographs with Qwen-Image 2.1 (8-bit, MLX-Serve), and the narration with VoxCPM2 (Apache-2.0) from a written voice description. The engine was shared with another session developing a new runner during the run, which the log shows and the timing notes account for. Every asset was made on this Mac with local models and tools. The generated project, transcripts and engine logs stay on the workstation.
