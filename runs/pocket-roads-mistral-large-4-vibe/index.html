Playable alpine time trial with both camera modes and the supplied textured coupe. The virtual chase view reads well. The physical-toy view shows dark road over flat dark ground with little scenery, so it gives a weak sense of movement.

What was checked

  • The model's browser check passed 35 of 35 in its session and again in an operator rerun on a clean clone of the final commit (ce328a0), in headless Chrome for Testing 154 from a nested path.
  • The check drives touch, keyboard, pause, calibration, camera anchoring and audio settings through real input events. The complete lap uses a built-in autopilot at six times game speed, and the collision checks place the car next to a hazard through a test hook.
  • The model also opened the build in the operator's desktop Chrome 158 and in the Vibe in-app browser. No iPad or physical touch screen was tested.
  • All 16 supplied runtime asset, library and notice files match the seed byte for byte.

Limits of this evidence

One operator message (the opening prompt). Smart approve ran 336 tool calls without asking and skipped five; the operator approved six calls when asked, including writing the model's Chrome DevTools Protocol client. The model installed no packages and wrote its own CDP client for Node 24. The capture is the virtual-mode frame from the model's own check.
