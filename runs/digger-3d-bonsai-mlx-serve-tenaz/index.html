Session partial Local run

Stopped by the operator after 19 h 21 min with 12 of 28 task cards done and no playable game: the simulation core was built (grid, digging, movement, gold bags, pathfinding, enemies, firing, spawner, scoring, levels and the tick), but no rendering, input or screens. Every task was accepted on its first review. At a 42,598-token session budget, 23 of 52 sessions ended early and builders kept reaching a second context compaction, which Tenaz stops; the run used three Tenaz builds and one manual kill of a hung check.

What was checked

  • Bootstrap: the spec step passed on its first session. In the verify step the model's ./verify --all looped forever (an argument loop without shift); Tenaz build c5c7450 had no tool-call watchdog, and the operator killed the check at 17:44 UTC after 22 minutes. That session then stopped at its second compaction and the next one passed. The task-card step was interrupted once by the first binary switch and then accepted: 54 requirements, 24 tasks.
  • Tasks: T001-T011 and T012a done, each accepted by a fresh reviewer on the first review; T012a took 3 attempts. T012 (the authoritative simulation) used all three context continuations and was split into T012a-d; T012b failed three attempts at the second compaction and was split into T012ba and T012bb. Two replans added nothing.
  • At the stop T012ba was active: two attempts had stopped at the second compaction, its check passed after the second, and its reviewer session was interrupted by the stop. T012bb, T012c, T012d and T013-T024 (app lifecycle, input, audio, terrain and camera, models, effects, HUD, title and game-over screens, integration, the 28 self-tests, a browser walkthrough and the final report) were never started.
  • Of 52 sessions, 23 ended early: 14 at a second compaction, 6 above the context budget (prompt plus an over-limit reply, 56,857-60,118 tokens; Tenaz continued them in fresh sessions) and 3 at operator pauses. Three times (T005, T011, T012) the over-limit reply was a whole-file write that Tenaz cut off before Pi ran it, so the continuation started over; build 9b45a03 runs such tool calls before ending the session.
  • Five of the second-compaction stops were retried as engine errors under builds c5c7450 and 7a7da2d, although the request had been cancelled by Tenaz itself; build 9b45a03 counts them as aborts. The engine served every request it was sent; the 12 error and 3 aborted requests were cancelled by Tenaz.
  • The last committed checkpoint passes 13 project checks in Tenaz's own verify (14 with T012ba's uncommitted check). The checks were not rerun independently, and there is no game screen to play: the page is still T001's skeleton scene.

Limits of this evidence

The 24 GB setup is a simulation on the 64 GB machine: it reproduces MLX-Serve's admission budget (16 GB of models, 3 GiB reserved for the OS, one resident model), a small prefix cache and the 64K context chosen for that budget. It does not reproduce a 24 GB Mac's memory bandwidth, GPU wired-memory limit (53 GB here) or real memory pressure: the server process grew past 16 GB to 19-25 GiB, and swap stayed below 0.8 GiB with normal memory pressure until the last twenty minutes. The drafter was not chosen for this model: MLX-Serve's automatic setting loaded the Qwen 3.8 27B pack's DFlash2 drafter through a link in Bonsai's folder (3.8 GB on disk; MLX-Serve's preflight estimated 1.01 GB), and DFlash took priority over PLD on every request. Not a clean single-version run: three Tenaz builds were used, fixed during the run for defects it exposed (no tool-call watchdog, an over-limit write lost before it ran, and Tenaz's own aborts counted as engine errors). No build of this attempt is published.
