Animated Pagoda over MCP

Session completed Local run

Tenaz ended in COMPLETE after 13 h 26 min with no failed task: Qwen 3.8 Flash Next built an animated seven-tier pagoda at dusk in a running Blender over MCP and delivered final.blend and a 7.5-second 1280x720 Cycles MP4. 16 tasks over 70 sessions; the project's verify passes 22 checks, and 9 of the workload's 11 hidden cases pass. The two that fail are the floors for an ultra-detailed pagoda and a complex scenario: 23.4k vertices in the pagoda (floor 100k) and 6.8k in its surroundings (floor 50k). The pagoda reads clearly, with lit windows and a rising camera, but it is low-poly and the setting is sparse. One operator action: resuming Tenaz after the app that had started it closed; the model restarted the MCP Blender and server itself.

What was checked

  • Bootstrap was accepted on its first pass: 23 requirements and 15 tasks, from a skeleton and inspection tools through the base, seven tiers, per-tier detail, finial, materials, environment, camera, secondary animation, render settings, stills, polish, the full render and the docs. A replan added T016 (dusk light, environment and framing). Reviewers returned CHANGES 6 times (T002, T010, T011, T013, T015, T016); one reviewer session (T001) crashed on non-UTF-8 output. T016 took three attempts and T002, T010, T011, T013, T014 and T015 two each. The final review accepted everything on its first cycle with verify passing.
  • How the scene was built: builders made 83 calls to the MCP server's tools (76 execute_blender_code, 4 get_blendfile_summary_datablocks, 1 get_blendfile_summary_path_info, 1 get_objects_summary, 1 execute_blender_code_for_cli) and 22 more through a small HTTP client the model wrote after the restart, against 296 shell commands that started a Blender of their own (149 ./blender, 135 ./verify, 12 render scripts). The scene is built by scripts/build_*.py run inside the MCP Blender and saved by scripts/save_scene.py. MCP tool calls stop after session 041 except 3 in session 051; from session 056 (T014 onward) no session touched the MCP Blender, and final.blend was last saved at 20:36 UTC.
  • The interruption: at 17:22 UTC the app that had started Tenaz, the MCP Blender and the MCP server closed. Tenaz recorded a pause and the operator resumed it at 17:27 UTC. The MCP Blender and server were gone. In builder sessions 045 and 047 (T013) the model found port 9191 closed, read the add-on's source, and started Blender with its window open on final.blend with -c blender_mcp, then blender-mcp --transport http --port 9191, both detached with setsid; the brief says not to quit or restart that Blender, and here it had already gone. Those processes served the rest of the run. Sessions started after that used the model's own client script (22 calls) more than the MCP tools (3 calls).
  • The final render ran as a Tenaz job (render/loop.sh, 9 min 22 s): Tenaz unloaded the LLM from Sushi for the job and loaded it again after, so Cycles did not share GPU memory with it. Three requests still ran out of GPU memory: one in T014 just after the job, and two in the final review, one of them while the reviewer's ./verify --all was rendering in the background. Tenaz retried each session without counting an attempt.
  • Independent check after the run: ./verify --all passed 22/22 in 90 s. The workload's 11 hidden acceptance cases, read from final.blend in Blender 5.2.1 and from the MP4: 9 pass. The pagoda has 7 tiers of eaves narrowing from 15.68 m to 6.49 m and is 44.76 m tall; 15 materials on rendered faces; 7.5 s at 24 fps, with the geometry, camera or lights changing in 12 of 12 sampled steps and 10 other animated datablocks; H.264 1280x720, 180 frames at 24 fps, not blank, frames differing over 7.3 s. Fail: pagoda.ultra-detailed (23.4k vertices in 818 loose parts; floors 100k and 150) and scenario.extent (6.8k vertices in 117 loose parts over 20.2 times the footprint; floor 50k vertices).
  • Seen by eye: a seven-tier wooden pagoda on a stone base with steps, dark tiled roofs with upturned corners and a bronze finial, against an orange-to-blue dusk sky; windows light up through the film while the camera rises from ground level up the tower. Trees are simple low-poly shapes, lanterns are small and the hills are bare. It reads as a pagoda at once, but not as ultra detailed, and the scenario is not immensely complex. Not checked: the .blend opened and reangled in Blender's interface.

Limits of this evidence

First run of Animated Pagoda over MCP, a prompt Ivan Fioravanti posted on X with a result from DeepSeek v4.1, Blender MCP and DeepSeek Harness; that result is a reference to look at, not a baseline. The run used an unreleased Tenaz build and has one operator action, both described in the workload notes. The SSD prefix cache was not emptied before the run, so the cache share is not a cold-start figure.
