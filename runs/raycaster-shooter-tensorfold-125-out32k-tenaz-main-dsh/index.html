Raycaster Shooter · TensorFold native 1.2.5, 32k output · Tenaz 36f2131 + 32k

Session partial Local run

Stopped by the operator after 4 h 0 min with 2 of 14 tasks done and no playable game, at the user's instruction to stop if the run would not finish within about 12 hours. With a 32,768-token output limit the bootstrap passed on the first try (30 requirements, 14 task cards), where the two starts before it with a 16,384-token limit blocked at the task-card step. T001 and T002 (scaffold and map; DDA walls and game loop) were done, T002 on verify alone because both its reviews stopped at the output limit without a verdict. T003 (wall textures and shading) failed twice: its own checks passed, but it broke T002's fisheye check both times; the third attempt was cut by the stop.

Measurements ↓

What was checked

  • Runner state at the stop: phase LOOP, paused at 10 November 03:37:51.640 UTC after the operator sent SIGINT; session 012 (T003 attempt 3) was cut after 4 turns. Tasks: 2 done, T003 active with 2 failed attempts, 11 todo. Then TensorFold got SIGTERM. Figures match tenaz status for the paused project (12 sessions, 1 stopped; 199 engine requests, all with engine timings).
  • Runner: an experimental local build of Tenaz main 36f2131 with one uncommitted, unreviewed change, HARNESS_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS 16384 -> 32768, so dsh asked for up to 32,768 output tokens. It reports 'tenaz 0.6.0 (36f2131)' like the unmodified build; SHA-256 of the binary e7b797d31317029224f41f841ce6f267cc14eb865d1146814d417372e63d393e. Every proxy record shows max_tokens of at most 32,768. Tenaz parameters at their defaults (65% context budget, 85,196 tokens; 40-turn budget; three attempts; review on); vision on, thinking medium.
  • Bootstrap: three sessions, each step accepted on its first attempt. Step 3 (task cards, session 003) ran 41 min 33 s and 50 turns, was steered on context pace and on turns, and ended on stop: 85,515 output tokens (63,112 reasoning), no reply at the limit, two replies longer than 16,384 tokens (20,558 and 23,457). In the two earlier TensorFold starts on this brief, with a 16,384-token limit, the runner blocked after three failed attempts at this step with no task cards written. An earlier run that day with the unmodified build, a 2 GiB prompt cache and the 16,384-token limit passed this step on its second try.
  • T001: attempt 1 (004) wrote no T001 check, and one of its replies used all 32,768 tokens in reasoning; attempt 2 (005) wrote two checks and passed verify (3 checks); review (006) returned ACCEPT. T002: one builder session (007) passed verify (5 checks). It had one reply at 32,768 tokens and ended on a reply cut at a 1-token limit (dsh had lowered max_tokens across that turn), so Tenaz ran the next sessions (008-011) at low reasoning effort. Both reviews (008, 009) ended on a 32,768-token reasoning reply with no verdict, and Tenaz accepted T002 on verify alone.
  • T003: attempt 1 (010) ended on a reply at the 32,768-token limit after 101,821 output tokens (87,969 reasoning). After it and after attempt 2 (011), T003's three checks passed but verify --all failed T002's fisheye check ('wall top edge bulges: spans rows 36..52 (16px variation, need < 3)'), word for word the same both times. No conflict note was written. Attempt 3 (012) was cut by the stop.
  • Why it was stopped (operator's notes): the loop averaged about 65-70 min per task; finishing the 12 tasks left within 12 hours would have needed about 40 min each, and they were the harder ones (sprites, enemies, weapons, HUD).
  • Engine metrics: all 199 proxy responses match TensorFold 'done' summaries one-to-one by (prompt, cached, output) in the single server log; matching from the start and from the end agrees, and the engine TTFT, decode time and draft counts in the proxy records equal the log's. 6 'done' summaries are unassigned: the preflight requests. The request in flight at the stop has no proxy record; TensorFold logged it as ended by the client after 18,083 tokens.
  • Memory: Tenaz's 30-second samples during sessions show pressure normal in 500 samples and warning in 1, swap in use 1.47-1.53 GiB, 2,848 pages (about 0.04 GiB) swapped in across the run, TensorFold's footprint at 49 GiB and free memory down to 0.11 GiB. TensorFold logged three memory-pressure warnings, each followed by a return to normal.
  • No public artifact: the run did not finish, so no build is archived. The prompt is the author's and is not republished; the project, raw logs, journal and sessions stay in the ignored local archive.

Limits of this evidence

Third TensorFold start of the night on this brief and the first of the three to get past bootstrap. The start just before it used the same Tenaz commit unmodified, the same 6 GiB prompt cache and a 16,384-token limit, and blocked at the task-card step. This run raised the output limit on both sides: TensorFold's --max-tokens and, through a local uncommitted Tenaz change, the max_tokens dsh asks for. Bootstrap then passed on the first try, with two replies longer than 16,384 tokens. The larger limit was not always enough later: five replies used all 32,768 tokens in reasoning, and both T002 reviews ended that way without a verdict. The Tenaz build is not a release and cannot be rebuilt from a public commit; its binary is identified by SHA-256. One execution, stopped unfinished, so its pace is no measure of what a full run would take.
