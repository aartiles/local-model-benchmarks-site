Tenaz Explainer

Session completed Local run

Tenaz reached COMPLETE in 12 h 24 min with no operator action: a 45-second silent HyperFrames video explaining Tenaz, scripted, designed and edited by Qwen 3.8 Flash Next, with five pictures it prompted and FLUX 3 Image Klein drew. 15 tasks over 54 sessions; verify passes 38 checks and 15 of the workload's 16 hidden acceptance cases pass. Slot image-5 is missing: four seed-105 renders were too dark, which spent all 10 calls. Every scene repeats one layout, a headline beside a small picture plate.

What was checked

  • Bootstrap was accepted on its first pass: 72 requirements and 14 tasks; the first replan added T015 (the task card travelling the loop). Reviewers returned CHANGES 6 times. Three builder sessions stopped at the 60-turn limit (T005, T015, T007); the T005 one had already passed verify and was accepted. The final review accepted on its first cycle, with 12 requirements marked manual.
  • generate_image was called 10 times, all in the T002 builder session, one at a time: every call returned an image. Five are used (slots 1, 2, 3, 4 and the optional 6). Slot 2 was retried once; slot 5 was tried four times and dropped because each render's luminance SD (10.0, 10.0, 11.95, 7.19) was under the brief's 12. The brief asked for one or two calls in reserve.
  • Tenaz's journal records, for each image, the LLM unloaded through Sushi's API (0.3-1.1 s), the ComfyUI job (90.2-94.2 s) and the LLM loaded again (7.8-8.2 s); the Sushi log shows the model unloaded and ready again at 44.2 GB resident each time. No request failed across the swaps.
  • The model opened a picture, still or contact sheet in 36 of 54 sessions. The T002 reviewer opened the contact sheet of the returned pictures, every task reviewer that reached a verdict opened stills of its scene, and the final reviewer opened 13 sheets and frames.
  • Independent check after the run: ./verify --all passed 38/38 in 44.5 s. The workload's hidden acceptance cases passed 15/16: format (1920x1080, 30 fps, 45.000 s, no audio), the five loop labels in order before the end card, the end card's exact texts held still from 43.05 s to 44.95 s, at most 25 words per frame, reading time, safe area, five pictures byte-identical to the image tool's output, 10 manifest calls matching 10 journal jobs with the right seeds and sizes, all pictures used by the composition, and unchanged vendored files. pictures.slots fails: image-5.png is missing. story.loop-in-order was corrected after the run, because it took a caption ending in 'Build.' for the Build label; it then passed on all 34 diagram frames.
  • Seen by eye: the seven beats are in order and every line traces to the fact sheet; the five pictures share one palette and lighting and show no letters. Every scene uses one layout, kicker, headline and caption on the left and a small framed picture or panel on the right, with most of the frame empty; pictures are never shown full frame. In scene 4 the check badge reads DONE in red, then turns amber, with no green. The travelling task card is 44x30 px and partly hidden behind the step plates. Not checked: whether a first-time viewer understands Tenaz from the video alone, and phone playback.

Limits of this evidence

First run of Tenaz Explainer and the first Tenaz run that unloads and reloads the LLM through Sushi's load API for each image. Sushi is 1.3.0 here, not the 1.2.4 of earlier Sushi runs, and the SSD prefix cache was not emptied before the run, so the cache share is not a cold-start figure. One hidden acceptance case (story.loop-in-order) was corrected after the run; the correction is described in the outcome.
