What was checked

  • Codex session records Chromium and WebKit checks covering acceleration, touch input, calibration, a timed finish and replay. No physical iPad was tested.
  • The archived build was independently checked for start and keyboard acceleration before publication.
  • Runtime assets and notices supplied to the Sushi attempt match the archived build byte for byte.

Limits of this evidence

The session records Qwen Image 2.1 and TRELLIS.2 work through MLX Serve. The opening plate, skyline and tree and car references came from Qwen Image. TRELLIS.2 generated the textured car from a masked reference. The run includes no recorded Blender production step. The capture comes from a later automated drive of the published build.
