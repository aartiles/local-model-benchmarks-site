Custom Characters Promo · TensorFold native (Zig) 1.0.12

Session partial Local run

The orchestrator ended COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS after 8 h 36 min of run time. 18 tasks are done and the project's verify passes 25 checks, but the second and last final review asked for three more fixes that were not made: captions cover the call-to-action button and the result card's name, and a label overlaps the form footage. The render passes 15 of 20 hidden acceptance cases. It leaves out the generation wait and the dark call screens, which the Opus 5.5 render of the Spanish brief did not, but its captions vanish in pauses and two of its texts are hidden.

What was checked

  • Bootstrap was accepted on its first session: 32 requirements, 12 task cards and a verify script. T001 (the composition skeleton) was later split twice into 5 smaller tasks.
  • Each task was accepted only after verify --all and verify --task <ID> passed and a fresh reviewer returned ACCEPT. One review returned CHANGES (T010). Final review cycle 1 added T013 and T014; cycle 2 added T015, T016 and T017, which the two-cycle limit left undone.
  • verify covers ./hf lint and ./hf check, the asset hashes, the chosen source windows against the timings in the notes, a draft render's size, frame rate and length, and stills at each beat and spoken sentence for the reviewers.
  • Independent check after the run: verify --all passed 25/25 in 55 s (limit 240 s). The committed render is 1080×1920, 30 fps, 20.000 s with audio, and was made after the last composition change.
  • Hidden acceptance cases: 15 of 20, read from the render with ffprobe, macOS Vision OCR at 4 frames a second and SSIM. Failed: sentence 3's caption collides with the result card's name; captions are hidden in two pauses (2.75–4.0 s and 8.25–8.75 s); the 'Pick a voice' label is partly covered; the caption plate covers 'Create yours at PapaNoel.chat'; the last caption fades out during the final hold. Each failure was confirmed on the frames.
  • Visual judgement (operator, not a score): the story reads beat by beat and the footage windows are correct, but the footage is small on a large dark field and the text collisions are visible. It is plainer than the Opus 5.5 render of the Spanish brief, which had a background per scene, a numbered label and headline in every scene and offset gold shadows, and was made with a storyboard, the hyperframes-creative skill and seven revisions with a person.

Limits of this evidence

Three fixes were made during the run, each logged with a marker in the server log and the run log. (1) TensorFold 1.0.12 decodes JPEG only with libjpeg-turbo 3.1.4.1 and Homebrew had 3.2.0; the operator built 3.1.4.1 into a private prefix and set TENSORFOLD_LIBTURBOJPEG in the LaunchAgent. (2) Sessions read more images than one request may hold (4 by default, up to 28 seen); the server was restarted with --vision-max-images 24 --vision-image-tokens 16384. (3) The orchestrator now continues a session in a fresh one when the engine refuses an image (count, size or format, such as the supplied WebP banner), with the refusal in the new session's prompt; before that change it retried the same session, which under greedy decoding failed the same way until the run blocked. The journal records orchestrator-changed twice, paused twice and image-continuation twice. The supplied footage, narration, generated project, transcripts and engine logs stay on the workstation.
