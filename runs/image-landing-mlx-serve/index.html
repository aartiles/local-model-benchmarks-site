26 October 2026 · Agentic task loop · web page with generated images

Image Landing · Qwen Image 2.1 · MLX-Serve 26.10.3

Session partial Local run

Tenaz reached COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS in 3 h 22 min with no operator action: the Fernwell landing page with five photographs that Qwen 3.8 27B prompted and Qwen Image 2.1 drew, both served by one MLX-Serve process that swapped the two models in memory. 10 of 11 tasks over 29 sessions, none failed; 6 image calls made 5 images, and verify passes 16 checks. The one open task only reorders the image manifest. The page works at both widths, but the product changes from image to image and the reviewers rarely looked at the photographs.

What was checked

  • Bootstrap was accepted after one retry of its third step, which first left R12 without a task: 32 requirements and 9 tasks. T001 needed two attempts (a 404 in the console); every other task passed on its first attempt. Two replans added no tasks.
  • The first final review returned CHANGES and added T010, to record the failed hero call in the manifest. The second returned CHANGES and added T011, because the manifest numbered the hero retry before the three cards it followed. Tenaz allows two final cycles, so the run ended COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS with T011 open. Both final reviews left R6 (no text), R7 (one visual family), R16 (palette from the images) and the aesthetic half of R25 as manual, saying they had not viewed the images.
  • generate_image was called 6 times: the first hero call failed with 'McpSessionExpired' before any job, and the model repeated it after the three cards; 5 images were made and all are on the page. The optional sixth image was not made.
  • During the image jobs /v1/models showed the LLM evicting or unloaded and Qwen Image loading or ready; the four T002 images ran back to back with Qwen Image kept loaded, and the LLM came back on the next chat request. No request was refused for memory.
  • The agent opened image files 9 times in the run, mostly page screenshots. The T002 reviewer and both final reviewers planned to look at the photographs but did not open them; only the T003 reviewer opened the background. In the 2026-10-25 FLUX run the agent opened images 48 times and the T002 reviewer rejected two.
  • Independent check after the run: verify --all passed 16/16 in 28 s. A separate script found the five assets are the required slots at their sizes, each byte-identical to a file the image tool wrote, 5 tool output files for 5 image jobs, no <svg>, <canvas> or remote URL, and one data: URI (the favicon). In Chrome at 1440×900 and 390×844 there were no console errors or failed requests, every <img> loaded with alt text, the background image loaded and nothing overflowed.
  • Seen by eye: the copy agrees everywhere (€129, six pods, six named herbs, refill every three weeks). The images share one palette but not one product: a sage tray of six pots on a board with soil showing, a glass water tank in an oak frame with five pots, a long ceramic base under a curved oak lamp at night with soil in the holes, a hero with an oak arch over five pots, and a background with soil in the pod holes; the copy says six pods and soil-free. On desktop the hero text sits in a narrow column with large gaps over a darkened photograph, and the footer's dark panel is narrower than the page. Not checked: keyboard navigation in detail and a real phone.

Limits of this evidence

Compare the images with the 2026-10-25 run as two image models, FLUX 3 Image Klein through ComfyUI and Qwen Image 2.1 on MLX-Serve, not as two engines for one model; the text side also moved from TensorFold to MLX-Serve, with different sampling and context. Memory was handled by the server (one resident model), not by Tenaz, which recorded 'lease llm kept'. The published build is the run's final commit; the site replaces its <title> and adds sharing metadata in <head>, and the images are byte-identical to what MLX-Serve returned.
