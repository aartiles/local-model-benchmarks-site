Custom Characters Promo · Sushi 1.2.4

Session completed Local run

The orchestrator reached COMPLETE after 3 h 55 min with no operator action and no failed session, and the final reviewer accepted all 27 requirements on its first cycle. The render passes 19 of 20 hidden acceptance cases: every caption in sync, both exclusions, the full call to action and a still final hold. It hides captions in the pauses between sentences. Visually it is clean but plainer than the Opus 5.5 render of the Spanish brief.

What was checked

  • Bootstrap was accepted on its first session: 27 requirements and 13 task cards. T001 (the skeleton) was split twice; one of its parts (T001aa, writing the narration timings) blocked after three attempts and a replan dropped it, its work done by a sibling.
  • Each task was accepted only after verify --all and verify --task <ID> passed and a fresh reviewer returned ACCEPT. Reviewers returned CHANGES on T001c, T006, T009 and T012. The final reviewer accepted on the first cycle.
  • Independent check after the run: verify --all passed 28/28 in 24 s (limit 240 s). The committed render is 1080×1920, 30 fps, 20.000 s with audio, and was made after the last composition change.
  • Hidden acceptance cases: 19 of 20. Failed: captions.always, captions hidden at 2.75–4.0 s and 8.25–8.75 s. The TensorFold attempt hid captions in the same pauses; the brief asks both for captions 'each while it is spoken' and 'always visible', so revision 2 should say which.
  • Audio: the music bed is ducked to 12% under each sentence and rises to 35% in the pauses; the chimes sit at 9.8 s and 15.9 s.
  • Visual judgement (operator, not a score): clean, readable and free of text collisions, but plainer than the Opus 5.5 render: one dark background for every scene, headlines only in the hook and the call to action, and few finishing details. Opus worked from a storyboard with the hyperframes-creative skill and seven revisions with a person; this run had neither, and the brief asked for restraint.

Limits of this evidence

Two changes from the earlier Sushi runs: Sushi 1.2.4 (faster Flash Next expert kernels, image-aware prefix cache) and the n-gram table. Following the model author's advice, the pack's 4-bit n-gram table (29.8 GiB, sha256 c8ab74bc…) was replaced with the bf16 table of the Sushi-4bpw pack, and config.json's ngram_table was set to 16 bits as the pack's README describes; the pack reports 3–5% lower error for it. Sushi cannot hold the 95.4 GB table in memory and reads rows from the SSD. Within this run the two changes cannot be told apart, and the workload differs from earlier Sushi runs. The rendered video is published with its narration made by Fish Audio Drama 3 (Built with Fish Audio). The footage, generated project, transcripts and engine logs stay on the workstation.
