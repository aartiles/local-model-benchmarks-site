Prefix Cache Anatomy

Session completed Local run

The orchestrator reached COMPLETE after 10 h 26 min of run time in three segments. All 14 model-planned tasks are done, the project's verify passes 35 checks in 103 s (limit 240 s), and the final reviewer accepted all 26 requirements. The model invented a paper-tape telegraph relay desk whose parts show prefix reuse, partial reuse, a miss, LRU eviction and refusal. 21 of 62 sessions ended on engine memory errors; none counted against a task.

What was checked

  • Bootstrap was accepted on its first session: 26 requirements, 14 task cards, full requirement coverage and a deterministic verify.
  • Each task was accepted only after the orchestrator ran verify --all and verify --task <ID> and a fresh reviewer returned ACCEPT. Reviewers sent T004 and T007 back once and T008 twice.
  • verify covers model.mjs behaviour, both page builds, headless-Chrome loads without console errors (the skill's capture.mjs), the skill's geometry audit, the line-end check (0 loose ends on both pages), and dark/light screenshots at 1440 px and 390 px plus one mid-motion frame.
  • The final reviewer drove the pages itself: the four prompt cases by pointer and keys 1-4, the idle tour, pause offscreen, reduced motion, and readouts matching a Node replay of the model.
  • Independent check after the run: verify --all passed 35/35 in 103 s; the screenshots are non-blank and show the machine and live readout; model.mjs was exercised outside the figure for reuse, partial reuse, miss, LRU eviction, oversized refusal and the 1.2 GB size of a 15,000-token state. One deviation: a full miss is charged the 0.3 s resume time on top of the prefill time.

Limits of this evidence

Run configuration changed between segments. Before the second segment the orchestrator gained a context cap driven by the engine's reported maximum, a large-tool-output steer, and a shared rule against reading large files whole (journal records templates-refreshed and orchestrator-changed). The cap never triggered, because every later engine error reported a maximum below the 32k floor. The generated project, transcripts and engine logs stay on the workstation.
