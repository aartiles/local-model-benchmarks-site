Lotion Pump Turntable

Session partial Local run

Tenaz ended in COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS after 21 h 15 min: Qwen 3.8 Flash Next built BlenderWorld task bw_0103, a frosted-glass lotion pump bottle turning once in a 6-second Cycles render, entirely from Python scripts, and delivered final.blend and a 1080x1080 MP4. 20 tasks over 93 sessions; the project's verify passes 32 checks, and 18 of the workload's 19 hidden cases pass under revision 2 (16 under revision 1). The open gap is documentation: STATE.md and PLAN.md describe the project before its last task. The backdrop still darkens to mid-gray at the top, against the task's light-gray-to-white. The final MP4 was rendered by the operator, with the model's own script, while the run was blocked: every render the model started inside a session collided with the LLM for GPU memory.

What was checked

  • Bootstrap was accepted on its first pass: 48 requirements, 14 tasks. A replan added T015 (separate the bottle from the backdrop). Reviewers returned CHANGES 5 times (T003, T009, T016d twice, T016c). The first final review (02:00 UTC) delivered a complete project and asked for T016 (a light-gray-to-white backdrop) and T017 (glass that reads cyan). The second (17:12 UTC) accepted everything but the handover docs and added T018, which the two-cycle limit left undone.
  • T016 took 11 h: three failed attempts, then a split into T016a-c; T016b failed three more and split into T016d-f. Ten builder sessions hit the 60-turn limit. T016f declared, under Tenaz 72ff5ab, that its thresholds conflicted with three done checks, with measurements: the lit wall tops out at 0.78 display luminance and reaching 0.90 clips the product 4.5-130 times over the T009 limit. The replan dropped T016f rather than amend those checks, citing the spec.
  • 14 sessions ended when Sushi ran out of Metal memory mid-generation (14 GenerationOutOfMemory in its log, each in a request sent while a Blender render the model had started was still running); fx reported ProviderError and stopped, Tenaz logged failed (toolUse) and retried without counting an attempt. After seven in a row during T017's 180-frame re-render, Tenaz blocked the run (engine unavailable). With the run blocked and the engine idle, the operator ran the model's own render_loop.sh unchanged: 165 frames in one pass and its encode script, 41 min. Tenaz was then resumed and the model checked and closed T017 itself.
  • Independent check after the run: ./.tenaz/verify --all passed 32/32 in 43 s twice; a first run passed 31/32 because T016d.backdrop-probe printed nothing (no error in its output). Hidden acceptance cases under revision 2: 18/19, failing only video.light-neutral-backdrop (darkest corner 82/255). Under revision 1: 16/19; label.blank-area counted both faces of the exact 50 x 40 mm label panel (4,019 mm2) and bottle.body-size measured the whole glass part (134 mm) where the model had made a 105 mm straight wall with a 13 mm shoulder and a 16 mm neck above it.
  • BlenderWorld's own rubric for bw_0103, read by hand (8 binary criteria, dataset revision e67837a7; not a BlenderWorld score, which uses cloud judges, quality scores and Blender 4.2.10): 5 pass. R03 a blank 50 x 40 mm label; R05 a 50 mm lens looking down 18 degrees in a 3/4 view; R06 one turn at exactly 2 degrees per frame about the origin with a seamless loop; R07 H.264 1080 x 1080, 6.000 s, 30 fps; R08 an editable final.blend with Cycles that the MP4 was rendered from (not tested: whether Blender 4.2.10 opens a 5.2.1 file). R02 likely fails: the 24.1 mm neck and 150 mm height are right and the nozzle does hook down, but the head reads as a plain cap and the anisotropic metal shows no visible brushing. R04 likely fails: three soft area lights, but the backdrop falls to mid-gray (82/255) in the upper corners. R01 is doubtful: 40 mm across with a 106.6 mm straight wall, but the glass part is 134 mm tall.
  • Seen by eye: a clean frosted light-cyan bottle with a rounded shoulder, a white label turning with it, a silver pump head, a soft floor shadow and a 3/4 view from 18 degrees above, turning at a constant 2 degrees per frame with no jump at the loop. The pump reads as a wide cap with a short inverted-U spout hugging its side rather than a press-pump with a nozzle reaching out, and the brushed finish shows as smooth gradients. The upper corners fall to mid-gray. Not checked: the .blend opened and reangled in Blender's interface.

Limits of this evidence

First run of Lotion Pump Turntable, a BlenderWorld task used as a challenge; it is not a BlenderWorld score, as BlenderWorld's evaluator was not run. The run spans three Tenaz builds and has one operator action, both described in the workload notes and the outcome. The SSD prefix cache was not emptied before the run, so the cache share is not a cold-start figure.
