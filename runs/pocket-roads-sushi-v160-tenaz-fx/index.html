Playable time trial. Tenaz stopped at COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS after two final review cycles. The off-road terrain looks flat at the finish and in a physical-mode braking view, and the chalets read as dark silhouettes.

Play build ↗Compare attempts →Measurements ↓

What was checked

  • 36 tasks done and two pending. The second final reviewer returned CHANGES for terrain and chalet readability (T037 and T038).
  • All 57 model-written checks passed in the final review and in an independent rerun of the frozen commit in headless Chrome. The suite covers both camera modes, a complete race, collision penalties and replay.
  • Touch inputs were exercised in headless Chrome. Physical iPad Safari and physical audio output were unverified.
  • All 16 supplied runtime asset, library and license files match the frontier build byte for byte.

Limits of this evidence

The public package contains static runtime files from the final commit. Its HTML head receives archive metadata. The gameplay still is a scripted virtual-drive frame from the independent check rerun. The binary reported Tenaz 0.7.0 (64ae2d7). The later Tenaz v0.8.0 release at 2f16791 ships an identical runner source tree under crates/.
