What was checked

  • Runner final state COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS on 20 November 10:23:19.353 UTC: 16 tasks done, T015 split into T015a and T015b, T016 to do. The verify bootstrap step failed once (verify --all printed no JSON last line) and passed on its second attempt; spec and tasks passed on their first (29 requirements, none manual). Final review cycle 1 returned CHANGES and added T015; cycle 2 returned CHANGES and added T016, which Tenaz's two-cycle limit left open. `verify --all` passed 17 of 17 model-written checks again after the run; not a hidden acceptance suite.
  • Configuration: identical to base run 1 and to pi-adapted run 2 apart from the checkpoint (same brief by SHA-256, Tenaz main 2f1b04c binary, Pi 1.4.0, thinking off, temperature 0.8, vision, generic engine, Tenaz defaults, sandbox off). Launched with --allow-weak-engine; the preflight reported reasoning=false cache=true tools=true vision=true.
  • Runner events: 32 of 78 sessions ended early: 26 at the turn budget, 3 after 20 turns without changing or reading anything new, 2 for repeated bash calls and 1 over its 85,196-token context budget. 24 turn-budget stops were continued in a fresh session. Three replans added no tasks. No response stopped at the output limit; 1 request ended in a provider error with no tokens.
  • The agent left 386 of its own scratch files (probe scripts and screenshots, many measuring the wall bow) in the ignored .tenaz/private folder.
  • Played on 20 November by the operator. The verdict: practically unplayable; the mouse turns far too fast to aim, and the walls are badly laid out.
  • Causes, checked by Claude in the source: turnPlayer in src/player.js multiplies each pixel of mouse movement by TURN_SPEED = 2.2, a value src/config.js documents as radians per second, so one pixel turns the view about 126 degrees. A flood fill of the grid in src/map.js from the spawn, through open floor and doors, reaches only the spawn corridor (46 of 183 floor cells): the big room, the three side rooms and the second corridor have no door that leads to them. The spawn view shows flat walls bowing outward, the fault T016 was added for.
  • Vision: no image refusals.
  • Engine metrics: a fresh Splash process serving only this run. Its 2,996 summaries are 6 preflight requests and 2,990 run requests. 2,990 of the 3,022 proxy records match a Splash summary one-to-one and in order by (prompt, output, cached); the other 32 are empty records (31 aborted turns, 1 provider error). Per session, the totals equal the Pi usage Tenaz journaled.
  • Memory: Tenaz's 900 host samples run from start to end. Wired memory went from 26.0 GiB to about 43 GiB (median 42.5, maximum 46.0). Pressure was at warning in 431 samples from 05:45 UTC, with other work on the Mac, and never critical; 0.1 GiB was swapped in.
  • Public artifact: the reviewed static build (vite build --base ./ from the final project commit 6539c0b, unchanged source) and its spawn view at 1280x720. The prompt is the author's and is not republished; the project, raw logs, journal and sessions stay in the ignored local archive.

Limits of this evidence

Completion is the runner outcome. A repeat of base run 1. It got further (no block) but did not finish clean, needed about 7 times the requests of pi-adapted run 2, and produced an unplayable game. With two executions per checkpoint, the pi-adapted model finished three of three runs cleanly and the base model none of two.
