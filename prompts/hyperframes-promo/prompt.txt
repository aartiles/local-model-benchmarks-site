# Custom Characters Promo

Edit one 20-second vertical promo video for PapaNoel.chat with HyperFrames, from
the footage and audio supplied in `assets/`. Use the HyperFrames skills: they
are loaded in every session, and read-only copies are in `.agent/skills/`. Read
`hyperframes-core/SKILL.md` before planning, and `hyperframes-cli/SKILL.md`
before running any command. Read their `references/` files only for the step
you are working on, and read large files by section, not whole.

## Scope

- **One composition, rendered to `renders/promo.mp4`.** 1080×1920 portrait,
  30 fps, exactly 20 s, with the supplied narration, music and chime mixed in.
- **No questions and no approvals.** Nobody will answer. This brief is the
  approved brief, and the render is approved in advance: render when the
  checks pass. Every creative decision inside these rules is yours.
- **Offline.** There is no network. The HyperFrames CLI is installed in the
  project: run it as `./hf <command>` (for example `./hf lint`, `./hf check`,
  `./hf render`), never `npx`. Do not use `usage`, `catalog`, `add`, `tts`,
  `transcribe`, `auth`, `publish`, `cloud`, `lambda` or `cloudrun`: they need
  the network or an account.
- **Local assets only.** GSAP is in `vendor/gsap.min.js`; load it with a
  relative path. Montserrat and Space Mono are in `vendor/fonts/` with
  `@font-face` rules in `vendor/fonts/fonts.css`. Use no other fonts, no CDN
  and no remote URL.
- **Do not change the supplied files.** Never edit, re-encode, trim or overwrite
  anything in `assets/`. `assets/SHA256SUMS` must still match at the end. Pick
  parts of the footage with the framework's media windows (`data-media-start`,
  `data-duration`, `data-playback-rate`), not with new files.
- **Inspect the footage yourself.** Use `ffprobe` and `ffmpeg` to read the
  sources and to extract still frames into `.agent/private/`, and look at the
  frames to find the parts you need. Write the timings you choose in your notes.

## The message

"Your child draws it. We bring it to life, and it talks with them."

A short, warm vertical promo for PapaNoel.chat, for parents and families. The
story goes from the transformation (the child's drawing comes to life), to how
simple it is for the adult to create, and ends on the emotional moment of
talking with it. Festive, clean and premium; the benefit comes before the
explanation.

## Assets

All are in `assets/`. Each is described by what it shows, not by its timings.

- `source/form-custom-character-creation.mov`: a real screen recording of the
  creation flow (silent). Use the upload, the form sped up, and the finished
  character card. It contains a long wait while the character is generated;
  leave that wait out completely.
- `source/vladdy-custom-character.mov`: a real video call with Vladdy, the
  character made from the drawing (silent). Use the middle of the call; leave
  out the dark intro screen before it and the dark thank-you screen after it.
- `source/custom-drawing-hero.mp4`: the drawing coming to life, for the hook.
- `source/child-pip.mp4`: the child's self-view for the call. Muted and
  mirrored.
- `source/child-pip-poster.webp`: the poster and fallback for that self-view.
- `source/before-after-banner-mobile.webp`: the drawing beside the finished
  character, with no text in it, for the call to action.
- `audio/voiceover-en.wav`: the narration, 20 s, five sentences (below).
- `audio/music-bed.mp3`: an instrumental music bed, 20 s.
- `audio/ringtone-magic.mp3`: a chime. Use only short pieces of it, as accents
  for the call connecting and the call to action.

## Narration (locked)

The voiceover says these five sentences in this order. Captions show the same
words exactly, each while it is spoken:

1. What if your child's drawing could come to life?
2. Upload their drawing, give it a name and tell us what it's like.
3. And we bring it to life.
4. Now it can talk, listen and have a conversation with your child.
5. Turn their drawing into a moment they'll never forget.

Find when each sentence is spoken from the audio itself (for example with
`ffmpeg`'s `silencedetect` filter). The pauses between sentences are about a
second long; shorter pauses fall inside a sentence, at its commas. Always write the brand as "PapaNoel.chat".

## Structure

Five beats:

| Beat | Time | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | The drawing comes to life. Headline: "What if their drawing could talk to them?" |
| Create | 3–8 s | The upload at about normal speed, then only the useful form fields at 4–5×. Labels: "Upload", "Describe", "Pick a voice". |
| Result | 8–10 s | The finished card "Vladdy, el vampiro bonachón" appears as the reward. |
| Call | 10–16 s | Vladdy on the video call, with the child's self-view on top. |
| Call to action | 16–20 s | "Imagine their face when they see it.", "Turn their drawing into someone to talk to." and "Create yours at PapaNoel.chat", held still for at least the last second. |

The recordings show the app's real interface, which is in Spanish; leave it as
it is. Everything you add (headlines, labels, captions, the call to action) is
in English.

## Rules

- **Captions** are always visible and in sync, in one lower band on a dark
  translucent plate.
- **Safe area:** keep faces, interface and essential copy inside the central
  80% of the frame.
- **The self-view** follows the site's hero treatment: a card at the top right
  of the call, with an amber border, the poster as fallback, muted, mirrored
  with `scaleX(-1)`, and clearly secondary to Vladdy. Place it over the empty
  self-view tile that the call recording already shows.
- **Audio:** the narration at full level; the music bed low under it (duck it
  under the voice); the chime as short accents only.
- **Do not invent** teeth, lips, gums, anatomy or features for Vladdy or the
  drawing. Show the supplied images as they are; do not crop the artwork.
- **Look:** a dark Christmas brand. Almost-black cranberry background
  (`#171018`), warm ivory copy (`#fff8ed`), red (`#d9283f`) and green
  (`#1f6f57`) for structure, gold (`#f3b43f`) for magic and interaction.
  Montserrat for headlines and body, Space Mono for labels. Headlines 90 px or
  larger, body and captions 32 px or larger. The footage is the focus; overlays
  are translucent and editorial, never clutter.

## Checks

`verify` must, within 240 s:

- run `./hf lint` and `./hf check` and require both to pass;
- check that `assets/SHA256SUMS` still matches;
- check every source window you chose against the timings in your notes (the
  wait and the dark call screens left out);
- render a draft and check its size, frame rate and duration with `ffprobe`;
- extract still frames at the middle of each beat and of each spoken sentence
  into `.agent/private/frames/`, and list them as artifacts, so that a
  reviewer can look at them.

The final task renders `renders/promo.mp4` at normal quality (outside
`verify`'s time limit if needed, with the CLI's detached render) and checks it
the same way.
