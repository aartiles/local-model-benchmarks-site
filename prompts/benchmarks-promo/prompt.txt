# Local Model Benchmarks Promo

Edit one 30-second vertical promo video for the Local Model Benchmarks website
with HyperFrames, from the footage, images and narration supplied in `assets/`.
Use the HyperFrames skills: they are loaded in every session, and read-only
copies are in `.agent/skills/`. Read `hyperframes-core/SKILL.md` before
planning, `hyperframes-cli/SKILL.md` before running any command, and
`hyperframes-creative/SKILL.md` before designing a scene. Read their
`references/` files only for the step you are working on, and read large files
by section, not whole.

## Scope

- **One composition, rendered to `renders/promo.mp4`.** 1080×1920 portrait,
  30 fps, exactly 30 s, with the supplied narration.
- **No questions and no approvals.** Nobody will answer. This brief is the
  approved brief and the storyboard below is the approved storyboard; the
  render is approved in advance. Every creative decision inside these rules is
  yours.
- **Offline.** There is no network. The HyperFrames CLI is installed in the
  project: run it as `./hf <command>` (for example `./hf lint`, `./hf check`,
  `./hf render`), never `npx`. Do not use `usage`, `catalog`, `add`, `tts`,
  `transcribe`, `auth`, `publish`, `cloud`, `lambda` or `cloudrun`: they need
  the network or an account.
- **Local assets only.** GSAP is in `vendor/gsap.min.js`; load it with a
  relative path. Archivo and IBM Plex Mono are in `vendor/fonts/` with
  `@font-face` rules in `vendor/fonts/fonts.css`. Use no other fonts, no CDN
  and no remote URL.
- **Do not change the supplied files.** Never edit, re-encode, trim or
  overwrite anything in `assets/`; `assets/SHA256SUMS` must still match at the
  end. Pick parts of a clip with the framework's media windows
  (`data-media-start`, `data-duration`, `data-playback-rate`), not new files.
- **Inspect the assets yourself.** Use `ffprobe` and `ffmpeg` to read them and
  to extract stills into `.agent/private/`, and look at them. Read only PNG or
  JPEG images, and combine several stills into one contact sheet rather than
  reading many.

## The message

"A laptop, a local model and enough time can build real software on their
own, and this site records exactly how it went."

A promo for Local Model Benchmarks, a public archive of what small language
models running on one laptop build from a single brief, with every run
measured and every build checked by hidden tests. The audience is developers
curious about local models. The tone is calm, precise and confident, like a
good technical documentary: the evidence is the hook, not hype.

## Assets

All are in `assets/`.

- `site/`: the website itself. `scroll-home.mp4`, `scroll-builds.mp4` and
  `scroll-run-sheet.mp4` are 6.5 s eased scrolls at 1080×1920; `home-mobile.png`
  and `run-sheet-mobile.png` are phone-width pages; `charts.png` (the
  side-by-side bar charts), `measurements.png` (a run's measurement table) and
  `builds-side-by-side.png` are desktop crops; `build-microwave.png`,
  `build-prefix-cache.png` and `build-hardball.png` are three builds the
  models made (two interactive figures and a 3D game).
- `figure/`: "Fig 1 · Local benchmark rig", an isometric machine that shows
  how a run works (task cards fed one at a time, the model writing, a verify
  gate, a reviewer's ACCEPT stamp, a done tray, a sealed box of hidden tests).
  `rig-loop-dark.mp4` and `rig-loop-light.mp4` are seamless 9.6 s loops at
  1080×1080; `rig-loop-transparent.webm` has an alpha channel; two 2160×2160
  stills. Keep `LICENSE-ANATOMY.txt` with it.
- `images/`: four 1080×1920 photographs: `laptop-night.png` (an open laptop
  under a desk lamp at night), `aluminium-edge.png` (a laptop edge in blue
  light, the top two thirds empty), `cubes-assembled.png` (glowing cubes placed
  by thin robot arms), `sunrise-desk.png` (the closed laptop and a coffee at
  sunrise: the night's run is done).
- `audio/narration-en.wav`: the narration, 30.000 s, six sentences (below).

## Narration (locked)

The voiceover says these six sentences in order. Captions show the same words
exactly:

1. What can a small model on a laptop build, if you give it time?
2. One MacBook Pro. Sixty-four gigabytes. Models running locally, with no one stepping in.
3. Games, interactive figures, even videos like this one.
4. Every run is recorded: every session, every token, every second.
5. And every build is checked against hidden tests it never sees.
6. Local Model Benchmarks. See what your machine can do.

Find when each sentence is spoken from the audio itself (for example with
`ffmpeg`'s `silencedetect` filter at about 0.6 s). Each caption appears when
its sentence starts and **stays on screen until the next sentence starts**, so
there is always one caption visible; the last stays to the end. Write numbers
in captions as spoken ("Sixty-four gigabytes").

## Design system

The video must look like the website.

- **Colours:** paper `#FAFCFE`, ink `#0A0E12`, body ink `#25292F`, muted
  `#4B5056`, hairline rule `#D4D8DD`, capture dark `#07090D`, accent blue
  `#1A48BC` (text on it `#FAFCFE`). No other hues except inside the footage.
- **Type:** headlines in Archivo 800, 96–120 px, letter-spacing −0.04em,
  line-height 1.02. Body and captions in Archivo 500–600, 34–40 px. Labels,
  numbers' units and the URL in IBM Plex Mono 500–600, uppercase for labels,
  tracked 0.09em, 26–30 px. Big numbers in Archivo 800.
- **Shapes:** square corners and hairline rules, as on the site. Footage sits
  in frames with a 2 px ink or paper border and a solid offset shadow of
  16 px in the accent blue (on light scenes) or in ink (on blue and dark
  scenes). The phone frame is a plain rounded rectangle (radius 48 px) with a
  thin border, no fake device details.
- **Layout:** label at the top left, headline under it, footage in the middle,
  caption band at the bottom. Keep all copy inside the central 80% of the
  frame.
- **Captions:** one lower band on a plate (ink at 85% on light scenes, paper
  at 92% with ink text on dark and blue scenes), centred, never covering a
  headline, a number or the footage's key content.
- **Motion:** calm and precise. Ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for
  entrances, ease-in-out `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` for moves, 0.4–0.8 s.
  Each scene enters with one clear move (a wipe, slide or reveal in the accent
  colour), never a plain cut, and the elements inside it stagger in. No bounce,
  spin or blur effects.

## Checks

`verify` must, within 240 s:

- run `./hf lint` and `./hf check` and require both to pass;
- check that `assets/SHA256SUMS` still matches;
- check the caption windows against the sentence timings in your notes;
- render a draft and check its size, frame rate and duration with `ffprobe`;
- extract stills at the middle of each scene and of each spoken sentence into
  `.agent/private/frames/`, combine them into one contact sheet, and list the
  stills and the sheet as artifacts, so that a reviewer can compare the scenes
  with the storyboard and the design system.

The final task renders `renders/promo.mp4` at normal quality (outside
`verify`'s time limit if needed, with the CLI's detached render) and checks it
the same way.
