Hardball 3D — generated-asset remake

Archived result of the 11–15 November 2026 local benchmark.
Runner outcome: COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS (exit 3); operator quality assessment: unsatisfactory.
The original gameplay code and its known defects are preserved.

Codex supplied the initial brief, specification, verification scaffold and task cards.
Qwen 3.8 Flash Next implemented the game through fx 0.0.16 and Tenaz 0.7.0,
using Sushi 1.6.0. Tenaz orchestrated the art generation as part of this run.

Player and glove concept images: Qwen Image 2.1, served locally by MLX-Serve.
Textured player and glove: TRELLIS.2 4B, served locally by MLX-Serve.
The agent made cutouts and reduced/recolored the generated meshes for rendering.
No downloaded game art, external audio or model weights are distributed here.
The two rendered GLBs are unchanged from the retained final build.
